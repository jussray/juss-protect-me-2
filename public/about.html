About Juss Protect Me 2: AI agent security

Juss Protect Me 2 (Model Two) is Juss & Co.'s AI-agent security project. It focuses on helping people set boundaries for AI assistants, connected tools, and automated actions so their systems remain under human direction.

Who is Model Two for?

Founders, developers, and teams exploring AI-assisted workflows who want clearer decisions about what an AI agent is allowed to access, recommend, or do.

How is Juss Protect Me 2 designed to work?

Model Two is being designed around defined permissions, trusted tool connections, and clearer action review. Its goal is to make automated work more understandable and accountable. A tool or system must be explicitly connected and verified; visiting this website alone does not activate protection.

Does Model Two already protect my AI tools?

Not automatically. This is an evolving early-access product, and public website availability does not establish live coverage of other agents, accounts, websites, or repositories. Enrollment has not opened. We distinguish planned capabilities from protection that has been independently demonstrated.

Is this the same as Juss Protect Me?

No. Model Two is focused on AI agents and their permitted actions. Juss Protect Me (Model One) focuses on personal online safety, devices, scams, and account guidance.

Who builds Model Two?

Model Two is developed by Juss & Co., founded by Raylene “Juss Ray” in Johnstown, Pennsylvania. We prioritize human control, privacy, understandable boundaries, and honest product status.
