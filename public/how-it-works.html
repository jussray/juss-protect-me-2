How Juss Protect Me 2 Works

Give AI agents useful capabilities without handing away control.

Juss Protect Me 2 is a security and oversight layer for AI agents and connected tools. Its approach is to define what an agent may do, check actions against those boundaries, and keep meaningful decisions understandable to people.

Explore AI security productsSee the products

The process in three clear steps

01

Set permissions and boundaries

Define which agent actions and tools are allowed for a particular task. Important actions should require appropriate authorization.

02

Check requests before action

When an agent is actually integrated with the gateway, configured checks can allow, reject or escalate requests. A web visit alone cannot connect an agent.

03

Review decisions and stay in control

Use the available evidence and activity records for supported integrations to understand decisions and improve permissions over time.

What is available, and what is not

Available to explore

The deployed site's edge shield protects this site's own Worker. The repository also contains gateway and governance components. Those facts do not prove that any unrelated AI agent, repository or customer service is connected or protected.

Requires future setup or enrollment

To protect an external AI agent, it must be intentionally integrated, configured, authorized and tested. Early-access enrollment is not open; there is no automatic protection just from viewing this page.

Frequently asked questions

Does visiting Juss Protect Me 2 protect my AI agents?

No. Site-level edge protection applies to this site. Other AI agents and tools require their own authorized gateway integration and verification.

What types of actions can the system review?

For supported integrations, the intended checks cover requested actions, trusted tools, permissions and content-safety boundaries. Actual enforcement depends on the connected system and configuration.

Can it act without my permission?

The design is based on explicit boundaries and accountable approval. It should not grant an agent broader authority than its configured permissions.

Is Model Two the same as personal device protection?

No. Model Two focuses on AI agent security and tool governance. Model One offers a guided checkup for everyday personal digital safety.
